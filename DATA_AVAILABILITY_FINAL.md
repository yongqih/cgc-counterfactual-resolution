# Data availability

Figure-level source tables are in `source_data/` and the Zenodo code archive (v2.0.0, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23148656). The Zenodo data archive (v2.0.0, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23148615) contains processed LARRY measurements, the 165-clone CellTag-multi early-feature matrix and held-out predictions, and Tahoe sufficient statistics for response-resolution and gene-selection analyses.

`DATASET_ACCESSION_MANIFEST.csv` identifies the public studies and data representations. `reproducibility/INPUT_DELIVERY.csv` lists the prepared inputs included in the archives and the additional inputs required for model fitting. Large study archives are available from the cited providers.
